Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Starforge Sales LLC ("we", "us") runs Grok for Closers at grokforclosers.com. This policy explains what we collect and what we do with it.

What we collect

Your name, email and phone number when you buy. What you share with us during the bootcamp: form submissions, homework, office-hours questions and messages in our WhatsApp group. Basic site usage data, such as pages visited. Our checkout provider handles your payment. We don't see or store your card number.

How we use it

To run the bootcamp, send session reminders by email, WhatsApp and text, give support, handle refunds, and send you emails about our programs. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time.

Your bots and your data

Your bots run in your own Grok account. The email, calendar, call notes and CRM data they read stay in your accounts and with xAI, under xAI's terms. We can't see that data unless you share it with us, for example in a homework submission.

Who we share it with

Only the services we use to run the bootcamp, such as payments, email, WhatsApp, video calls and texting. We don't sell your information.

How long we keep it

As long as we need it to run the bootcamp, keep business records and meet legal requirements.

Your choices

Email us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you.

Who this is for

The bootcamp is for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
